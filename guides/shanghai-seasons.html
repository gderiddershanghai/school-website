What is Shanghai's weather like through the year?

Flying English Center2 min readReviewed 20 October 2026
In this guide

  2. How does rain affect daily life?
  3. Should you choose housing based on the season?

← All questions

Shanghai has four seasons, with hot summers, cooler winters and a period of persistent rainy weather often called the plum rain season. Prepare for changing conditions rather than treating the city as warm all year.

The city's official seasonal overview describes that pattern. A seasonal guide is not a forecast: check current conditions before travel or a day out.

What should you wear when you arrive?

Choose clothes for the expected conditions during your first weeks, then use layers to adjust. Consider how much time you will spend walking, waiting outside and moving between indoor spaces.

A light outfit for a warm day may not be comfortable throughout an air-conditioned journey and workday. Likewise, judging winter clothing only by the daytime forecast can miss the experience of an early or late commute.

How does rain affect daily life?

Think about the whole route: an umbrella is useful, but so are footwear you can walk in comfortably and a bag that protects your documents and electronics.

Check where you can dry clothing in a prospective home. During damp weather, a small practical detail such as drying space may matter more than it did in a listing photograph.

Decision Weather-related check
Choosing a home Ventilation, drying space and heating/cooling arrangements
Choosing a commute Exposed walks and a route for heavy rain
Packing Layers, comfortable shoes and protection for belongings
Planning an outing Current forecast and an indoor alternative
Going to work Enough time to arrive comfortably and prepare

Should you choose housing based on the season?

View it with more than the current weather in mind. Ask how the heating or cooling works, test the equipment where possible and understand who pays for utilities. Consider sunlight, ventilation and the condition of windows.

Those checks belong alongside the payment and lease questions in the rental guide.

When should you plan a trip?

Begin with your actual days off and the forecast. Seasonal descriptions can help you choose clothing and activities, but they cannot promise a dry weekend or a comfortable temperature on a particular date.

Keep an alternative plan for a short trip rather than making every part of the day depend on outdoor conditions.

For the move itself, use the packing guide. For everyday travel, test your commute under the conditions you are likely to encounter.
