Teaching here

Small classes.
A hands-on school day.

  1. Teach children aged 3–12

    Classes have four to six students. Our curriculum includes ESL and Language Arts, with games, reading and practical activities.

  2. Start with training

    Pre-job training and ongoing support are part of the role. Teachers applying for their first position are welcome.

  3. Work after school and at weekends

    Expect 18–22 teaching hours a week and two consecutive weekdays off. We’ll discuss preparation time and the full schedule at interview.
