Est. 2015 · Huangpu, Shanghai

How Flying English took flight.

A boutique school built by people who wanted more for their kids — and ended up building a family of teachers from around the world.

Where it began

Two mothers, one idea.

Flying English is a boutique language school in downtown Shanghai, founded in 2015 by two mothers — a former chemical engineer turned entrepreneur and a business consultant — who weren’t satisfied with the after-school options around them. Together with an education consultant, they set out to raise the bar for young learners.

The route so far

Milestones, from take-off to today.

  1. 2014

    Opened our doors with 2 classrooms and 12 students.

  2. 2017

    Moved to our current home in Huangpu District.

  3. 2019

    Passed 500 enrolments, opened a second campus, and grew to a team of 40.

  4. 2020

    The pandemic closed borders — and, sadly, our second campus.

  5. 2026

    Looking for you to join the team. Next stop: late 2026.

Our mission

Stay small, teach big.

To remain a boutique centre delivering high-quality English education — while nurturing every child’s creativity, confidence, and curiosity.

What we’ve achieved

A decade in.

  • Taught 1,000+ young learners since 2015
  • Built a robust ESL & Language-Arts curriculum
  • Ran seasonal STEM and Arts & Crafts camps
  • Survived a global pandemic
  • Kept a close-knit team that stays for the long haul

More than coworkers

We travel together, too.

In 2024 we took the whole team to Bali for a week of fun and bonding. It’s part of how we keep teachers happy — and keep them around. When you teach here, you don’t just get a job. You get a crew.
