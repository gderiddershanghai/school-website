Est. 2015 · Huangpu, Shanghai

A small school in Huangpu.

Founded by two mothers looking for better after-school options for their children. Today, we teach English to young learners in classes of four to six.

Where it began

Why the founders started Flying English.

Flying English was founded by two mothers: a former chemical engineer turned entrepreneur and a business consultant. They wanted different after-school options for their children and worked with an education consultant to start the school.

Our history

How the school has changed.

  1. At the start

    The school started with two classrooms and 12 students.

  2. 2017

    Moved to our current home in Huangpu District.

  3. 2019

    Passed 500 enrolments, opened a second campus, and grew to a team of 40.

  4. 2020

    The pandemic closed borders — and, sadly, our second campus.

  5. 2027

    One advertised teaching opening for early 2027.

In the classroom

English through reading, games and projects.

We teach children aged 3–12 in groups of four to six. Alongside ESL and Language Arts, the school has run seasonal STEM and Arts & Crafts camps.

The school so far

From our school records.

  • Taught 1,000+ young learners since 2015
  • Developed an ESL & Language Arts curriculum
  • Ran seasonal STEM and Arts & Crafts camps
  • Continued at our Huangpu location after closing the second campus

Away from school

The team’s 2024 trip to Bali.

In 2024 the team spent a week in Bali. Our photo archive also includes dinners, laser tag and other outings. You can see more of those photographs on the teaching role page.
