# How do you pay for everyday things in China?

Prepare more than one way to pay. Mobile payments can be convenient, but card compatibility, verification and transaction type can affect what works. Test your setup before relying on it for an essential purchase.

Shanghai's [official payment guide](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-BankingandPayments-Payments/index.html) covers mobile payments, overseas bank cards and cash. Use its current instructions rather than an old screenshot of an app.

## What should you set up before travelling?

Install the official payment apps you intend to use and read their current requirements. Check whether your phone number can receive verification messages abroad and whether your bank permits the intended transactions.

Keep a second method available. A working card, some accessible cash and a charged phone serve different purposes; none should be assumed to work in every setting.

## Does linking a card mean every feature will work?

No. Being able to make one purchase does not establish that transfers, deposits, refunds or other functions are available in the same way. Check the terms for the transaction you need.

Before a large payment, confirm the recipient, amount, currency and any fees displayed. A merchant-payment workflow should not be confused with sending money to a person.

| Task | Useful preparation |
| --- | --- |
| A small shop purchase | Test a low-value transaction |
| Transport | Set up and test the relevant ticket or payment method |
| Accommodation payment | Ask which payment methods are accepted in advance |
| A refund | Keep the transaction record and check the provider's procedure |
| Losing phone access | Know how to contact your bank and payment provider |

## What if a payment fails?

Check the message before repeatedly trying. It may involve verification, a bank decline, a limit or the transaction type. Use the provider's official support route and a backup method when necessary.

Do not share passwords or verification codes with someone offering to fix the problem. When asking staff for help, keep control of your own account.

## Do you need a Chinese bank account immediately?

That depends on what you need to do and what your employer requires for payroll. Visitor-payment options and a long-term salary account are different arrangements. Ask what documents and account details are needed for your first salary before scheduling a bank visit.

This guide does not assume that one banking setup suits every newcomer.

## How does this fit into arrival planning?

Arrange payment access alongside [your phone setup](phone-internet-china.md), and keep offline copies of essential booking details. Use the [arrival-budget calculator](china-first-month-budget.md) to estimate what you need available before payday.
