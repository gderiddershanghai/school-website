# How to plan your first train trip in China

Choose a manageable journey, confirm the exact departure and arrival stations, and use the same identity document for booking and travel. Allow time for reaching the station and boarding, not just the journey printed on the ticket.

For a first trip during your days off, a simple itinerary with a confirmed return is easier to manage than several tightly timed connections.

## How do foreign visitors buy train tickets?

Shanghai's official guide describes booking through China Railway's 12306 service and using accepted identity documents, including foreign passports. It explains identity verification and boarding with the document used to buy the ticket. Read the [official train-ticket guide](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Transportation/20240110/f69efb3643094385a0efeb62f594200f.html) before booking.

Enter names and document details carefully. Complete any required verification early rather than discovering an account issue immediately before your intended departure.

## Which station do you need?

Check the full station name on each ticket. A city can have more than one station, and a convenient arrival station does not mean your return departs from the same place.

Save the station names and accommodation address in a form you can show someone if needed. Check the local journey at both ends.

| Part of the plan | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Outbound ticket | Date, train, station, passenger details and seat |
| Station access | Route, expected travel time and boarding allowance |
| Arrival | Distance from station to your first destination |
| Return | Departure station and enough time to reach it |
| Changes | Current ticket change and refund conditions |
| Documents | The accepted original document used in the booking |

## Is a short train ride always a good day trip?

No. Add the journey from home to the station, boarding time and travel from the destination station. A short train segment can still produce a long day.

For illustration, 40 minutes to the station, 40 minutes of waiting and boarding, a 60-minute train and 30 minutes onward already add up to 2 hours 50 minutes each way. Those are hypothetical planning numbers, not a real route.

## How should you choose a destination?

Pick one main reason to go: a museum, a neighbourhood walk or time with a friend. Check the attraction's opening and booking arrangements separately. Leave enough flexibility to enjoy the place rather than spending the day catching transport.

If your days off are weekdays, check those specific opening days. Do not assume an attraction follows the same schedule as your workplace.

Before leaving, review your [payment setup](paying-in-china.md), save your itinerary offline and check [the weather](shanghai-seasons.md). Keep the return journey compatible with your next working day.
