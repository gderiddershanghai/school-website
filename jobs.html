Practical questions

Before you apply.

What is the salary, and what is extra?

The base salary is RMB 21,500–25,000 per month, with a separate RMB 3,500 monthly housing allowance. Quarterly performance bonuses range from RMB 1,000–3,500 and are conditional, not guaranteed monthly pay. Contract completion bonuses and overtime are additional. Ask us to confirm the tax treatment and terms for your offer.

Do I need teaching experience?

Beginners are welcome, with pre-job training and ongoing support. The advertised requirements include a bachelor’s degree, a TEFL certificate completed or in progress, a clean background check and a commitment of at least one year. Contact the school to check your eligibility before making relocation plans.

Will I work at weekends?

Yes. Teaching takes place in the evenings and at weekends, with two consecutive weekdays off. The role includes 18–22 teaching hours per week. Ask for the full working schedule, including preparation and other duties, during your interview.

What help is available when I arrive?

The school offers work visa sponsorship, training and arrival support, including help with housing, banking and setting up a SIM card. Ask the recruitment team which costs are covered and what documents you will need.

How do I apply?

Email your CV or questions to flyingenglish@163.com. The advertised opening starts in early 2027.
